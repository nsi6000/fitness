## Full Workouts

### Workout 1 - Lifting
* Theme: Working on major lifts. Horizontal Extension with power. Burpees and thrusters/cleans/snatches for metcons. Barbell work. No pullup bar work. Light on the grip.

### Workout 2 - Gymnastics
* Theme: Working on gymnastics moves. Bodyweight and pullups/toes-to-bar for metcons (with optional Running). No Barbell work. Pullup bar work. Heavy on the grip (deadlifts, pullups, toes-to-bar). Abs/Core work. Wear a Weighted Vest for additional difficulty.
