
# Glossary

|Concepts|Meaning|
|--|--|
|WOD|Workout Of the Day|
|METCON|Metabolic Conditioning|
|The MGW Framework|Monostructural (M), Gymnastics (G), and Weightlifting (W). The 3 categories of exercises.|
|Couplet|Workout combining 2 of the 3 categories of exercises. (e.g. Fran with Thrusters (W) and Pullups (G).)|
|Triplet|Workout combining all 3 categories of exercises. (e.g. Helen with Run (M), KBS (W), and Pullups (G).)| 
|AMRAP|As Many Reps As Possible|
|EMOM|Every Minute On the Minute|
|E2MOM|Every 2 Minutes On the Minute|
|E3MOM|Every 3 Minutes On the Minute|
|TC|Time Cap|
|Tabata|20 seconds max effort, 10 seconds rest. For 8 cycles, 4 minutes in total.|
|Chipper|List of exercises with high rep counts that you complete sequentially and "chip" away a movement before moving to the next.|
|RFT|Rounds For Time|
|For Time|Workout should be completed within time frame.|
|Death By X|Ladder workout: Start at 1 rep on minute 0 and add 1 rep each minute until cannot complete within the minute.|
